# Plan

The orchestrator runs the `plan` skill on these triggers. With a product team, it sends the owner's words to product as `plan: <words>` instead.

- The owner gives an idea, a refinement or a change of direction, not a concrete task → `plan` skill, **fold**.
- The owner says "continue", "next" or "погнали дальше" → `plan` skill, **next**.
- A piece of work closes with a result, such as an experiment → `plan` skill, **review**.
